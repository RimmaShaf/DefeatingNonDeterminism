Each cell shuffles [1e-10, 1e-5, 1e-2, 1, -1e-10, -1e-5, -1e-2, -1] independently and sums it. Mathematically the terms cancel exactly to 0. In float64, the order in which tiny magnitudes meet huge ones decides how much precision gets silently eaten. The color encodes deviation from zero within the observed range.
